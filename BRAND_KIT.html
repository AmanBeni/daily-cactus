Brand & Style Kit

The visual system for the paper — minimal, premium, warm. Flat kraft ground, flat cream cards, classic Georgia type, one green accent, muted section colours, thin brown lines.

v3 · current

Principles

  • Restraint over decoration. Flat surfaces, soft shadows, no textures, no squiggles, no tape.
  • One accent. Riso green = brand (nav). Espresso brown = the warm voice + every line. Red = LEAD badge only.
  • Colour = topic. Muted section colours label the topic; page stays neutral paper.
  • Lines are thin & brown — section rules and the page's top/bottom rules, all 1.5px. No side borders.
  • Type does the hierarchy, one tight scale, generous space.

Logo

  • Papercraft cactus sits to the left of the green wordmark in the masthead, modest in size.
  • Clear space ≥ the pot's width. Never stretch, recolour, or add effects.
  • Small uses (colophon, favicon): the mark alone.
